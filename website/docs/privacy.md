# Privacy Policy — Anything

**Last updated: 14 August 2026**

Anything ("the app", "we", "us") is an on-device application that turns a
description you type into a working app on your phone. This policy explains
exactly what happens to your data. The short version: **Anything has no server,
no account, and collects nothing about you.**

## Who we are

Anything is an independent, open-source project.
**Contact:** dev@gratefulworldventures.in

## Data we collect

**We (the developer) collect no personal data.** There is no account, no sign-in,
no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Anything has no backend server of
its own — nothing about you is sent to us or stored by us.

Everything the app creates and remembers — the apps you generate, their data,
your settings, and your AI provider key — is stored **only on your device**.

## Your AI provider key (BYOK)

To generate apps, Anything uses an AI provider that **you** choose and configure
with **your own API key** ("bring your own key"). That key is stored locally in
your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore) and is **never uploaded to us**.

## Data you send to third parties

When you ask Anything to build or change an app, the **text of your request**
(and, if you use voice input, the text transcribed from your speech) is sent
over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the **AI provider you configured**
(for example, Groq) so it can generate a result. Your requests are processed
under **that provider's** privacy policy — please review it.

Apps you build may also fetch public information from the web (via the app's
"Discover" feature). These are ordinary web requests to public sources.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, because we never collect it
in the first place.

## Voice input

Voice input is **optional**. When you use it, speech is transcribed by your
device's built-in speech recognition. Audio is **not** sent to us or to the AI
provider — only the resulting text is used, exactly as if you had typed it.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — to reach your chosen AI provider and to let the
  apps you build fetch public web data.
- **Microphone** — only for optional voice input, transcribed on-device.

Anything does **not** request access to your location, contacts, photos, or
files, and does not use device identifiers.

## Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. **Uninstalling the app permanently deletes
everything** it created — there is nothing on any server to delete, because there
is no server.

## Children

Anything is a general-purpose developer/productivity tool and is **not directed
at children under 13**.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last
updated" date.
